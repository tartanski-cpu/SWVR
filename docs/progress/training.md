## Changes

### M1: the training harbour and five harbour exercises (30 Oct 2026)

**On `preview/training` for the owner to try.** Release notes: https://claude.ai/artifact/1YCyusD75iMebk1vM7DK5w (text: `docs/RELEASE_NOTES_2026-10-30_training.md`).

Owner, 30 Oct 2026, on the plan's questions: Training is its own area; the training boat is the fishing boat (with prop walk); the verdict is "passed / try again" first, the total underneath; English only.

- **The page and the mode.** `training.html` (a copy of `voyage.html`, its title Training) sets `TRAINING` in `src/mode.ts`. `MODE` stays `'voyage'` there, so Training is a Voyage in every respect, and above all the career-save guard holds: `careerSession.ts` already skips restore and save for a Voyage, so the training page can never load or overwrite a career slot. The map id is `training`; `world/regions/index.ts` answers it with the training harbour alone (it is not on the Voyage list). The page starts at Berth B every time (no remembered place), with no live weather (`startLiveWeather` finds no Voyage map), the duty board shown (Voyage hides it), passing traffic off, and the calls quiet. Crew joining a training host are sent to `training.html`. The front page's Training card now opens it.
- **The training harbour** (`src/world/regions/training.ts`): Harbour Head, the harbour cut into its north shore, 6 m of water throughout (a bank under it keeps the breakwater's rubble from spreading in from deep water); a breakwater with a 56 m entrance to the north, lit Fl R 4s / Fl G 4s, a lateral pair outside; a 260 m fendered quay with Berths A (fuel), B (the start) and C; the slot (two short jetties 25 m apart: about 6 m clear at each end of the fishing boat); the dock (two quay walls 30 m apart, three of her lengths, 32 m long, open to the north); the stopping lane (280 by 30 m between four yellow special marks); an office and a shed. The rest of the plan's harbour comes with the exercises that use it.
- **The exercise engine** (`src/training/training.ts`): the first-voyage tutorial's pattern made general. An exercise puts the boat in place, starts her engine and sets the wind (by the manual weather, a flat sea, daylight); its steps are announced over the VHF one after the other (each line waits for the one before to have been spoken: `voiceCharsPerS`, `voicePauseS`) and ticked on the duty board (a `training` duty, `duties.ts`). While it runs, faults are counted: over the speed limit (weight 1), a knock over 1 kn (2), and the fails (3: a knock over 2 kn, or the exercise's own broken rule) end the run there and then. Each kind of fault counts again only after `faultGapS`. It ends **passed** or **try again**, with the reason and the exercise's numbers; two attempts (`attempts`), Start again (not counted) and Stop. Plain JSON on `WorldState.training`, driven by intents (`trainingStart`, `trainingRetry`, `trainingRestart`, `trainingAbort`), so crew see it in their snapshots.
- **The exercises** (`src/training/exercises.ts`), each brief naming what it practises:
  - **H4 First cast-off** (RYA L1): lines off, out between the lane marks and back alongside any berth under 4 kn, engine off.
  - **H5 Stop on course** (SBF "kursgerechtes Aufstoppen"; RYA L2): down the lane at 4 kn; on "Stop her!" stopped within 15° of her heading, in the lane, lever back in neutral. Card: speed at the call, stopping distance, time, heading change.
  - **H6 Turn in the dock** (RYA L2; SBF "Wenden auf engem Raum"): round to within 10° of north inside the dock, under 3 kn, 8 kn of wind from the west; out of the dock before she is round fails.
  - **H8 Alongside, wind off** (RYA L2; SBF "Anlegen"): Berth B port side to, 8 kn off the quay: fenders out; under 1.5 kn one length off, under 45° at the first touch, a knock under 0.5 kn, neutral when the first line goes ashore, made fast at B heading west.
  - **H10 Leaving the slot** (RYA L2; SBF "Ablegen"): out ahead, then (put back in the slot) out astern, two lengths clear, her hull touching nothing.
- **The Training tab** (`src/training/trainingMenu.ts`, first in the menu on the training page): the exercises with Start and a tick when passed, what's running with Start again and Stop, the last verdict with its Second attempt button; the whole card (the reason, the numbers, the faults) under its Result section. The line "practice modelled on the RYA and SBF schemes: not a course, no certificate" at the top.
- **The training boat** (`boat: 'fishingBoat'`). The plan's audit was wrong about her prop walk: every Shipyard boat is built from the landing craft's recipe, whose shaft propeller walks (0.03 ahead, 0.12 astern, right-handed, the old workboat's figures); the zero it found is the outboard module's own. So no change was needed. Measured: a crash stop from 4.8 kn, wheel amidships, swings her 18° (H5 fails it); with the walk corrected (a kick ahead with opposite helm when she starts to swing), 7° in 8 m. Her slow ahead: 3 kn at lever 0.2, 4.7 at 0.3, so the 4 kn limit is fair. docs/TRAINING.md section 2 corrected.
- **Config keys** (`CONFIG.training`): `boat`, `attempts`, `harbourKn`, `knock.faultKn` / `failKn`, `faultGapS`, `stoppedKn`, `voiceCharsPerS`, `voicePauseS`, `weights`, and per exercise `stop` (H5), `turn` (H6), `alongside` (H8), `leave` (H10), `castOff` (H4).
- **Tests:** `tests/training.test.ts` (7): the page is a Voyage on the harbour alone in the fishing boat at Berth B; water where every exercise needs it, the breakwater and the land above it; each exercise places her, starts her engine, briefs over the VHF, puts its steps on the duty board; H5 driven down the lane and stopped, judged with its numbers; H6 failed by leaving astern, then its second attempt; H8's first step, Start again and Stop; a fault counted once per gap. `tests/menu.test.ts` stub given the two new host members. The typecheck and all of `npm test` pass (one long line hauler test timed out once with the browser running beside it; it passes on its own).
- **Seen headless** (Chromium): the front page; the harbour from the air and the quay; the menu's Training tab before, during and after an exercise; the Result card after a failed H6; the boat in the dock at the second attempt; the instructor's lines as VHF notices. No errors in the page.

**Try it**

- *Desktop:* open https://tartanski-cpu.github.io/SWVR/preview/training/ and click **Training**. You start on the quay beside the fishing boat. Press **O** for the menu: the **Training** tab is first. Press **Start** on **H5 Stop on course**: you're put aboard; press **F** for the helm. Listen to the brief, then **W** to about 4 kn down the lane; at "Stop her!" pull back to astern and watch her head swing to starboard (her stern walking to port); kick ahead with port helm to hold her. Open the menu again for the verdict, and **Result** for the card. Then **H6** in the dock, **H8** alongside Berth B (fenders out first; click the cleats to make fast) and **H10** in the slot.
- *VR (Quest 3):* the same address, **Enter VR**, the menu on your left wrist (**Y**). Start **H8 Alongside, wind off**: put the fenders out, bring her in to Berth B port side to, neutral, and reach out to the cleats for the lines. Check the duty board on the starboard wall aft ticks the steps, and the VHF speaks each line without cutting the last off. Note the frame rate in the harbour (Settings, the debug overlay) against the home world.
