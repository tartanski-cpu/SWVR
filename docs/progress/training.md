### M3: Harbour practice complete, Rescue practice, the Examiner's Run, and the missing mechanics (30 Oct 2026)

**On `preview/training` for the owner to try.** Owner, 30 Oct 2026: "Build it all, including missing mechanics" (springs, the mooring buoy, a chosen anchor scope, the kill cord: plan questions 3 to 7 all yes); badges mean nothing in the Career (the safe default, question 11); the existing boats for now ("continue as you can with some of the existing boats").

**The mechanics** (shared code, each small, each its own test in `tests/seamanship.test.ts`):
- **Springs** (`vessel/physics/mooring.ts` `toggleSpring`; the Boat tab's Springs row, Fore / Aft): a rope from her bow cleat aft to the berth's aft bollard (fore spring), or the stern cleat forward (aft spring). It pulls only stretched past its length, as stiff as the lines, and stays fast with the bow and stern lines let go, so she can motor ahead against it: tested, she swings her stern out 20°+ with her bow held. `CONFIG.docking.springSlack`.
- **The mooring buoy** (a new mark kind, `mooring`: a small orange pick-up buoy, unlit, drawn with the marks and on the paper chart): a forward cleat (or the Boat tab's Buoy row) makes fast to one within `buoyReach` (3 m) of her bow under `buoyMaxSpeed`, on a `buoyPennant` (4 m) rope; she lies to it downwind. `CONFIG.docking.buoyReach / buoyMaxSpeed / buoyPennant`.
- **Anchor scope** (`vessel/physics/anchor.ts`; the Boat tab's Scope row, 2:1 / 3:1 / 5:1 / 7:1): the windlass lets go to the scope chosen, veers more chain when it's raised with the anchor down, and heaves in to it (the anchor left down) when it's lowered. A chosen scope may be short (2:1), and its holding builds to full at 5:1 (`CONFIG.anchoring.chosenFullScope`), the seamanship rule; **unchosen, the anchor is exactly as before** (full from 3:1, the windlass's 5:1). Measured on weed in 24 kn: 2:1 drags over 2 m in two minutes, 7:1 under half a metre. A finding for the owner: in this model, past 3:1 the snatch on a long chain moves an anchor about as much as a shorter one holds it (5:1 and 7:1 behave alike); the exercise judges the scope chosen as well as the drag.
- **The kill cord** (`vessel/killCord.ts`; the Boat tab's Kill cord row, on the skiff and the RIB, `CONFIG.killCord.boats`): clipped on, it stops her motor when its wearer is more than `reachM` (1.5 m) from her helm or off her; then it's "pulled" until clipped on again. **In Training the motor won't run without it**; elsewhere it's the skipper's choice (unclipped, she runs as before).
- **The horn's signals** (training only, `training.ts trackHorn`): a press up to 2 s is a short blast, from 4 s a prolonged one; blasts under 2.5 s apart make one signal. `CONFIG.training.horn`.

**The harbour** (`world/regions/training.ts`): three anchorages outside the breakwater, on sand, mud and weed (6 to 9 m); six slalom marks; the hold-off mark; the mooring buoy; the search datum; the fuel pump at Berth A works.

**The exercises** (`src/training/harbourCourse.ts`, `rescueCourse.ts`, with the shared parts in `exerciseKit.ts`), 23 in all with milestone 1's:
- **Harbour:** H1 Start her up (cold and dark, in order, shut down); H2 Fuel her up (engine off, or it fails); H3 Lines and fenders (in the order called, a spring on and off); H7 Hold off the mark (90 s, off it no more than twice); H9 Alongside, wind on (12 kn onto Berth C; drifting on in neutral is a fault); H11a/b/c Anchor in sand, mud, weed (way off, head to wind, the scope chosen: 3:1 at least, 5:1 in weed; a drag over 3 m fails; motoring hard over the chain is a fault); H12 Horn and lights (after sunset: lights before leaving, then one, two, three short blasts before each manoeuvre); H13 Low-speed slalom (alternately, there and back, under 3 kn); H14 Springing off (12 kn on the quay: fore spring, lines off, ahead against it, stern out 30°, away astern); H15 Pick up the mooring; H5T and H6T, stop on course and the dock turn in the skiff with her tiller.
- **Rescue:** R1 Man overboard into wind, R2 drift down, R1T in the skiff (out of gear within 3 s, the stern turned away, up into the wind or beam on drifting down, recovered with the ring or the hook; in gear within 5 m of the swimmer fails); R3 Take her in tow (needs a towing post: the pilot boat); R4 Fire on a boat (needs a fire monitor: the pilot boat); R5 Fog in the harbour (a prolonged blast every two minutes, out by compass and back); R6 Alarm under way (the impeller fails: see the TEMP alarm, ease off within 20 s, stop her before she cooks); R7 Search and recover (an expanding square from the datum in fog, each leg called; then recovered).

The boat judged is the one the exercise uses (`training.ts trainingBoat`). An exercise that needs gear the boat lacks (R3, R4 in the fishing boat) says so and doesn't start.
