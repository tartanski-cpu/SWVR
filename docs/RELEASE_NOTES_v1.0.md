# Workboat World v1.0

**Play it:** https://tartanski-cpu.github.io/SWVR/ in the Meta Quest 3 browser (press Enter VR) or on a desktop browser. There's nothing to install.

Workboat World is a sea rescue and harbour-work game. You crew a chunky 12 m workboat from the inside: walk the deck, climb into the wheelhouse, start the diesel, and work real levers, switches, wheels and winches. You can play solo or with friends, peer to peer.

## Starting out

- You start on the quay at **Calder Haven**, beside your boat, which is moored at the cargo berth.
- New careers begin with a **first voyage**: the harbour master talks you through it over the VHF. You step aboard, power up, start the engine, load a crate, cast off and deliver it.
- Each browser holds **three career slots**. Money, reputation, upgrades and the boat's condition are saved.

## The boat

- **A 12 m displacement workboat.** She floats on proper hydrostatics, has a slow-revving diesel, gearbox and prop, and shows prop walk. She heaves, pitches and rolls in the swell.
- **Walk-in wheelhouse:**
  - wheel, throttle/gear lever, key, glow plugs and start button;
  - horn, lights and wipers;
  - a breaker panel (master, instruments, radio, bilge, deck hydraulics…).
- **Instruments:**
  - speed and depth, compass, engine RPM, rudder angle;
  - an overhead NAV / WIND display;
  - a chartplotter;
  - a paper chart on a fold-out chart table, which you can draw on;
  - a VHF radio, and binoculars on the dash.
- **Engine care:**
  - fuel (refuel at a fuel berth) and engine temperatures with alarms;
  - a cold start that smokes white;
  - the oil: open the engine hatch in the wheelhouse floor, check the dipstick, top it up.
- **Deck hydraulics** (engine running and DECK HYD breaker on): the skiff crane, the tow winch and the trawl's net drum, each with its own sound.
- **Mooring:** come slowly alongside a quay, then click the **cleats** forward and aft to send the lines to the bollards. Click again to let go.
- **Deck gear:** fenders, anchor and windlass, searchlight, a stabilised fire monitor on the roof, a swim ladder, D-rings to lash cargo.
- **The skiff:** a small electric boat on the crane. Launch it to land goods on beaches with no quay.

## Work

- **Rescues:** distress calls over the VHF. Find the survivors (strobes, whistles, waving), get them aboard with the life ring, line and hook, and land them at a quay. You can also plot the position yourself on the paper chart.
- **Towing:** broken-down cruisers, log rafts, work barges. Make fast with the boat hook over the stern roller and tow them into harbour. The tow winch heaves in and pays out. You can tow anything afloat, debris included.
- **Fires:** boats burning at sea and sheds ashore. Put them out with the fire monitor.
- **Cargo runs** between the ports. Goods are carried by hand and are real weight in her stability. Lash them down or they slide and go over the side.
- **Side jobs:** salvage logs off beaches, and trawl the fishing grounds and land your catch.
- **Career:** pay, reputation at each port, job boards, contracts with deadlines, and upgrades from the chandlery.
- **Wear and damage:** hull damage leaks into the bilge, the engine wears, and the yard repairs both.

## The world

- **A small archipelago:**
  - Calder Haven, Brannock Pier and Selkie Bay (an anchorage with a beach);
  - rocks and shoals, buoys and lights;
  - sheltered water inside the breakwaters.
- **Weather that changes:**
  - wind, swell, rain on the windows, squalls with lightning and thunder;
  - fog, with ships sounding their horns;
  - forecasts read out on the VHF.
- **Day and night,** with harbour floodlights.
- **Traffic:** coasters, fishing boats and yachts pass by. Watch for floating debris too.
- **Wildlife:** gulls, and dolphins riding the bow wave.
- **Off the boat:**
  - walk ashore, jump between boat and quay;
  - swim, and climb back up the ladder.

## Playing together

- One player hosts. Their game code is on the duty board in the wheelhouse, and crew-mates join with it.
- Everyone works the same boat and the same controls.
- Crew-mates play in the host's career.

## Known limits

- In very big rolls, sea water can show inside the hull.
- The hazard buoys don't block the boat.
- Nights are very dark (there's no moon yet).
- Beam seas make her roll quite a lot.
- In VR, the controller models need an internet connection to appear. Without it the hands still work, but you can't see them.
