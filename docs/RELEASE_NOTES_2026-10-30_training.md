# Workboat World Training: release notes, 30 Oct 2026 (milestones 1 and 2)

The published page: https://claude.ai/artifact/1YCyusD75iMebk1vM7DK5w (with the development screenshots and two things to try). Try it: https://tartanski-cpu.github.io/SWVR/preview/training/training.html

The third way to play has started. A small walled harbour built for practising boat handling, and five exercises modelled on the RYA Powerboat Scheme and the German SBF. The instructor talks you through each over the VHF, the steps tick off on the duty board, and each attempt ends passed or try again, with the reason and the numbers. It is practice, not a course: nothing here is a certificate.

## The speed log turns blue going astern (Helm)

The owner: "It's quite hard to tell if you're going forward or backwards on a computer." With more than 0.15 kn of sternway through the water, the SPEED LOG's figure and bar turn blue with ASTERN by the title (amber ahead). A crawl under that counts as stopped; drifting sideways is neither. A round LOG dial shows ASTERN beside it. Every boat has it.

## Every attempt recorded, and reviewed on the chart (Review, M2)

Each attempt is recorded as you go, kept in this browser, and opened from Training, then Runs.

- **Recorded:** ten samples a second (position, heading, motion, lever, rudder, revs, gear, fenders, lines, the knock, and where you were), the whole world every 30 s, and the events: steps done, faults with their weight, touches, lines, the horn, the instructor's lines, the verdict. A minute is about 40 KB as a file.
- **The review:** the run on the harbour's chart with the quays drawn; the track coloured by speed (blue at rest to red at 6 kn); pins at the steps (blue), faults (red, with the weight; a cross for the one that failed it), touches (orange), lines (purple); your other attempt grey underneath; the boat to scale where she was; a time bar with the events; that moment's numbers (speed, heading, lever, rudder, revs, distance off the quay, lines).
- **Stepping through:** back and on 10 s, previous and next event, Play at ×1, ×4 or ×10. Desktop: at the bottom left, drag along its time bar. VR: a panel in front of you.
- **Kept:** the last 40 runs in the browser's own store (not with the career saves). Save gives a file; Load a recording from a file (desktop) opens one.
- Measured, H5 twice: a crash stop with the wheel amidships, her head 18° off (try again); the walk corrected, 7° in 7 m (passed). The notes page lets you scrub the two recorded tracks side by side.

## Five exercises, each a few minutes afloat (Drill)

Menu, Training, Start: you are put aboard at the helm, the boat in place, the engine running, the wind set.

| No. | Exercise | Practises | Passed when |
|---|---|---|---|
| H4 | First cast-off | RYA Level 1: steer and control speed | Lines off, out to the lane, back alongside any berth, engine off, under 4 kn throughout |
| H5 | Stop on course | SBF kursgerechtes Aufstoppen; RYA L2 taking way off | On "Stop her!" from 4 kn: stopped inside the lane, heading within 15°, lever in neutral |
| H6 | Turn in the dock | RYA L2 turning in a confined area; SBF Wenden auf engem Raum | Round to within 10° of north inside a dock three lengths wide, under 3 kn, 8 kn of wind from the west |
| H8 | Alongside, wind off | RYA L2 speed and angle of approach; SBF Anlegen | Berth B port side to, 8 kn off the quay: under 1.5 kn one length off, under 45° at the touch, knock under 0.5 kn, neutral at the first line |
| H10 | Leaving the slot | RYA L2 leave ahead or astern; SBF Ablegen | Out ahead, then out astern, two lengths clear, her hull touching nothing |

- The instructor's lines come over the VHF one at a time, each waiting until the last has been spoken.
- The steps are on the duty board on the starboard wall aft.
- Faults: over the speed limit (weight 1), a knock over 1 kn (2). A knock over 2 kn, or the exercise's own rule broken, fails the run there and then.

## An examiner's verdict, not a score (Verdict)

Each attempt ends with the reason and the numbers that matter for it; the fault total underneath. Two attempts each; Start again is never counted. The Exercises page keeps the last verdict in a line with Second attempt beside it; the whole card is under Result.

## The training harbour (Harbour)

Cut into the north shore of Harbour Head, 6 m of water throughout: a breakwater with a 56 m entrance to the north (Fl R 4s, Fl G 4s, a lateral pair outside); a 260 m fendered quay with Berths A (fuel), B (the start) and C; the slot (two jetties 25 m apart, about 6 m clear at each end of her); the dock (two quay walls 30 m apart, 32 m deep); the stopping lane (280 by 30 m between four yellow special marks). No town, no harbour boats, no traffic, no calls.

## The fishing boat walks her stern after all (Boat)

The plan said every Shipyard boat had prop walk set to zero; that zero belongs to the outboard module. Every Shipyard boat is built from the landing craft's recipe, whose shaft propeller walks (0.03 ahead, 0.12 astern, right-handed). Measured in H5: a crash stop with the wheel amidships, 18° off her heading (try again); with the walk corrected, 7° in 8 m (passed).

## A Voyage with an instructor aboard (Page)

The front page's Training card opens training.html: a Voyage on the training harbour, so it never loads or writes a career save. It starts at Berth B every time, with no live weather. Crew who join are taken to the training page and see the same exercise.

## Try it

1. Open the training page. Desktop: O for the menu. VR: Y.
2. Training, Start on H5; F takes the helm on the desktop.
3. About 4 kn down the lane; on "Stop her!", astern, and kick ahead with port helm as her head falls off to starboard.
4. The menu for the verdict; Result for the card. Then H6, H8 (fenders out, the cleats for the lines) and H10.
